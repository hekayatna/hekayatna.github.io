FAQ

Everything you want to know

How fast will I get my invitation?

Within 24–48 hours after we receive your details and you approve the design.

How long does my link stay active?

Before and after the event. It stays online as a digital keepsake of your photos and blessings.

Can I change the time or venue after delivery?

Yes. We update the details on the same link, so guests always see the latest version.

Are guest messages and photos private?

Yes. They go to a private, password-protected dashboard that only the couple can open. You decide whether guests also see a blessing wall.

What can I customize, and how do I pay?

Names, colors, style, music and wording are all customizable. Pay by InstaPay, bank transfer or e-wallets.
